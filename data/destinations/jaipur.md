# Jaipur travel notes

These are sample planning notes. Costs are rough estimates, so check current prices.

## Areas
The Old City (Pink City) has the bazaars, City Palace and Hawa Mahal. Amer is about 11 km north and has the main fort. C-Scheme and MI Road have many hotels and restaurants.

## Things to do
Amer Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Hawa Mahal and Nahargarh Fort are the main sights. A composite ticket can reduce the cost of visiting several monuments. Entry fees are roughly 100 to 700 INR per person for Indian visitors depending on the site. Johari Bazaar and Bapu Bazaar are good for shopping. Evenings at Nahargarh give a good view over the city.

## Where to stay
Hostels are roughly 400 to 900 INR per bed per night. Budget hotels are roughly 1200 to 2500 INR per room per night. Mid-range and heritage hotels are roughly 3000 to 8000 INR per room per night.

## Food
Try dal baati churma, pyaaz kachori and lassi. Street food and local restaurants cost roughly 150 to 400 INR per person for a meal. A budget traveller can manage on about 600 to 1000 INR per day for food.

## Getting around
Autos and app cabs are the easiest way to get around. A day of local trips costs roughly 500 to 1200 INR. The metro covers only part of the city. A hired car for a full day costs roughly 1800 to 2800 INR.

## Best time to visit
October to March is the most comfortable. April to June is very hot, so plan outdoor visits early in the morning.
