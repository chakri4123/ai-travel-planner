# Goa travel notes

These are sample planning notes. Costs are rough estimates, so check current prices.

## Areas
North Goa (Baga, Calangute, Anjuna, Vagator) is busy and has more nightlife. South Goa (Palolem, Colva, Agonda) is quieter and suits relaxed beach days. Panaji and Fontainhas are good for Portuguese-era streets and cafes.

## Things to do
Beach time is free. Old Goa has the Basilica of Bom Jesus and Se Cathedral, with low or no entry cost. Fort Aguada and Chapora Fort are easy half-day visits. Dudhsagar Falls is a full-day trip and is best after the monsoon. Spice plantation tours usually include lunch, roughly 500 to 1000 INR per person.

## Where to stay
Hostels are roughly 500 to 1000 INR per bed per night. Budget guesthouses are roughly 1500 to 3000 INR per room per night. Mid-range hotels are roughly 3500 to 7000 INR per room per night. Prices rise sharply around Christmas and New Year.

## Food
Beach shacks and local restaurants cost roughly 300 to 700 INR per person for a meal. Try fish curry rice, prawn balchao and bebinca. A budget traveller can manage on about 800 to 1200 INR per day for food.

## Getting around
Rented scooters cost roughly 300 to 500 INR per day plus fuel and are the cheapest way to get around. Taxis are expensive compared with other Indian cities. Rental cars cost roughly 1200 to 2000 INR per day. Keep a valid licence and wear a helmet.

## Best time to visit
November to February has the most comfortable weather. June to September is the monsoon, when many shacks close but prices drop.
