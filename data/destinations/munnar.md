# Munnar travel notes

These are sample planning notes. Costs are rough estimates, so check current prices.

## Areas
Munnar town is the base for most trips and has the main market. Chinnakanal and Pothamedu are quieter with more resorts. Top Station is on the road towards Tamil Nadu and is known for its valley views.

## Things to do
Tea estates and the tea museum are the main attraction, with museum entry of roughly 100 to 200 INR per person. Eravikulam National Park is known for the Nilgiri tahr and is usually closed for part of the calving season. Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point and Top Station work well as a one-day circuit. Trekking with a guide costs roughly 800 to 2000 INR per person.

## Where to stay
Homestays are roughly 1200 to 2500 INR per room per night. Mid-range resorts are roughly 3000 to 7000 INR per room per night. Book early for December and January.

## Food
Kerala meals, appam with stew and fresh tea are easy to find. Local restaurants cost roughly 150 to 400 INR per person for a meal. A budget traveller can manage on about 600 to 1000 INR per day for food.

## Getting around
Most sights are spread out, so travellers usually hire a jeep or taxi, roughly 2000 to 3500 INR per day. Rented scooters cost roughly 400 to 600 INR per day. Roads are narrow and winding, so allow extra time.

## Best time to visit
September to March is the most pleasant. June to August brings heavy rain and occasional landslides.
